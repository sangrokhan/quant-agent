## Hypothesis

Classic 13-period EMA / 48-period EMA dual crossover, long-only, unfiltered.
Long when the 13-EMA is above the 48-EMA, flat otherwise. The source's own
backtest on the S&P 500 cash index (1960-present) found it invested ~66% of
the time, CAGR 5.1% vs. buy-and-hold's 7.2%, with a slightly better
risk-adjusted return (7.8%) and no filter found to improve it. Distinct from
every other EMA-crossover variant already in this repo, all of which add a
confirmation filter (OBV, RSI, volume, zero-lag/adaptive EMA, etc.) — this is
the plain, unfiltered classic construction, tested here as a distinct
baseline on modern QQQ/SPY/crypto data (2016-2026) rather than the source's
own S&P 500 cash-index sample.
